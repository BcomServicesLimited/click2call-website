Run from Queensland, upfront about the time difference

Click2Call is run from the Gold Coast, so it's worth being upfront about the time difference from the start.

Western Australia is on UTC+8 and doesn't use daylight saving. Queensland is on UTC+10, also without daylight saving, so Perth is two hours behind us all year. Our support line, 1300 884 879, is open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 5 pm Queensland time. In Perth, that's 7 am to 3 pm, every week of the year.

If something comes up after 3 pm Perth time, email support@click2call.com.au and the team will pick it up when they're next in. For the everyday changes, such as adding users, adjusting call flows or setting holidays, the portal is available around the clock and the help centre has a step-by-step guide for each one.

Answering east-coast callers before you open

The time difference cuts the other way too. When a Sydney customer rings at 9 am their time in summer, it's 6 am in Perth. Instead of ringing out, those calls can be handled automatically:

  • ✓Set your opening hours in the portal and mention 'Perth time' in your after-hours greeting
  • ✓Send early calls to the Answer Agent, which asks for the caller's name, number and reason, then emails you the details and a transcript
  • ✓Switch on missed-call alerts so the first person in can see who rang overnight
  • ✓If someone on your team starts early, forward the first hour of calls to their mobile

The Answer Agent is included free. You set the questions it asks and choose from 38 voices.

Perth numbers: 08, new or ported

Perth numbers start with 08, an area code shared with South Australia and the Northern Territory. Every user plan includes a local number, and extra numbers are $10 a month each.

To keep the number you already have, port it. Porting is $100 inc GST per number. Local numbers usually take 5–10 business days and 1300 or 1800 numbers up to 30 days. The number keeps working on your current service until it moves, so don't cancel anything until the port is confirmed.

Doing business on the east coast? Add an 02 or 03 number that rings your Perth team, so east-coast customers can call a number that feels local to them.

A worked example: a Perth office with east-coast clients

Picture a ten-person engineering consultancy in West Perth whose biggest clients are in Sydney and Brisbane. Here's one way to set it up:

  1. 1Two front doors. The main 08 number for WA clients, plus an 02 number ($10 a month) printed on proposals for Sydney clients. Both ring the same team.
  2. 2Early calls. Before 8 am Perth time, calls go to voicemail with the Answer Agent switched on, so it asks for the caller's name, number and reason and emails the details to the office.
  3. 3Business hours. From 8 am, calls ring the project managers together, with a call queue holding callers if everyone is busy.
  4. 4Office and site. Office staff answer on softphones or desk phones; engineers on site get calls forwarded to their mobiles.
  5. 5Records. Recording is switched on for the main numbers, so transcripts and summaries of client calls are on hand if a scope is ever disputed.

Every piece of that is a standard setting. You can build it yourself during the trial or ask for managed setup.

Remote sites, FIFO crews and Starlink

Western Australia's distances shape how a lot of businesses work. Head office is in Perth, but the people who matter might be in the Pilbara, the Goldfields or the South West, on a rotating roster.

A cloud phone system suits that. Staff on a remote site can use the Secure VoIP app or a softphone over the site's internet connection, including Starlink, and still make calls from the business number. Our help centre has a guide to getting good call quality over Starlink.

Where mobile coverage is reliable, forwarding calls to a mobile number is the most dependable way to receive them. Where it isn't, the Answer Agent or voicemail takes a message, and the details reach email as soon as there's a connection.

Ring groups can include crew in Perth and on site, so a call to the business number reaches whoever is available.
