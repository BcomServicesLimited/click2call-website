A phone system that suits how Brisbane works

Brisbane businesses are spread out. A head office in Fortitude Valley, a team in Springfield or North Lakes, tradespeople and reps on the road across Logan and Moreton Bay. A traditional phone system ties everyone to a building. A cloud system doesn't.

With Click2Call, the phone system runs in the cloud. Desk phones, the Secure VoIP app and softphones all connect to it over the internet, and you manage everything in a web portal: who rings when a customer calls, what happens after hours, who can see recordings.

So a call to your Brisbane number can ring the person best placed to answer it, wherever they are, and be transferred to anyone else without a second phone call.

Your 07 number, new or ported

Queensland has a single area code, 07, so a Brisbane number looks the same as one from Toowoomba or Cairns. Each user plan comes with a local number, and you can add more numbers for $10 a month each.

If customers already know your number, port it across. It costs $100 inc GST per number and local numbers usually move in 5–10 business days; 1300 and 1800 numbers can take up to 30 days. Leave your current service connected while the port goes through, because your number keeps working there until the moment it moves.

Storm season: keeping calls coming in when the power goes

South-east Queensland's summer storms are part of life, and so are the power and internet outages that come with them. When your office loses power, desk phones go dark, and with an old on-site phone system callers heard nothing at all.

Because Click2Call runs in the cloud rather than in your office, it can still answer your calls during an outage and send them somewhere useful. Set it up before the season starts:

  1. 1In the portal, open your main number and choose Call Forwarding.
  2. 2Turn on Forward when Offline and enter a mobile number.
  3. 3Save, then test it by disconnecting the desk phones and ringing your number.

When your phones drop offline, calls go to that mobile instead. Voicemail keeps working in the cloud too, so messages still arrive by email.

For extra cover, add a ring group that rings a mobile alongside the desk phones, so calls reach someone whether or not the office is online.

Getting your office internet ready for calls

Internet calls need a steady connection more than a fast one. Before you move your number, use the trial to check call quality on your own connection:

  • ✓Make test calls at your busiest time of day, while the office is using the internet as normal
  • ✓Plug desk phones into the network with a cable where you can, rather than relying on Wi-Fi
  • ✓If calls connect but audio only goes one way, turn off the router's SIP ALG setting; our help guide shows how
  • ✓If menus you call don't recognise your keypad presses, the DTMF guide covers the fix
  • ✓If your business can't do without its phones, consider a backup internet connection such as a 4G router, alongside Forward when Offline

Many offices on a standard NBN plan need no special configuration at all. If you're unsure, our support team can talk you through what to check.

Queensland time and interstate callers

Queensland doesn't use daylight saving, so for half the year Brisbane runs an hour behind Sydney, Melbourne, Canberra and Hobart. That's easy to forget when your customers are interstate.

Between October and April, a Sydney customer ringing at 8:30 am their time reaches you at 7:30 am. Your opening hours, set in the portal, decide what they hear: a greeting that gives your hours in Queensland time, the Answer Agent taking their details, or a call forwarded to whoever starts early.

Our own support runs on Brisbane time. The support line, 1300 884 879, is open Monday to Friday from 9 am to 5 pm all year.

Teams in the office, at home and on site

Most Brisbane teams now mix office days with home days, and many have people on the road. Each user can have the same extension on several devices: a desk phone at the office, a softphone on the laptop at home and the Secure VoIP app on their mobile.

When someone rings a customer back from home, the customer sees the business number rather than a personal mobile. For field staff who need incoming calls on the road, forwarding to their mobile number is the most reliable option. Forwarded calls are billed as outbound calls from the forwarding line.

Hot desking suits offices where people share desks: staff dial *45 on any desk phone to log in with their own extension.

Routing calls the way your business answers them

Ring groups

Ring the whole sales team at once and let the first free person answer.

Call queues

Hold callers with music and tell them their position while everyone is busy.

AI Receptionist

Callers say 'accounts' or 'service' and are put through, with no keypad menu. It routes calls; it doesn't hold conversations.

Answer Agent

If nobody picks up, an AI takes the caller's name, number and reason and emails you a transcript. It's included free.

Missed-call alerts round it out: any call that rings out sends a text or email to whoever handles callbacks, and the live Call Dashboard shows the day's missed calls at a glance.

Microsoft Teams and existing phone systems

If your business runs on Microsoft 365, Click2Call can connect Teams to the phone network through Direct Routing, so staff call from Teams using your Australian numbers. Each user needs a Teams Phone Standard licence from Microsoft, about $15 a month, as well as our $25 Teams User plan.

Already have a 3CX, FreePBX or Yeastar system you're happy with? Keep it and connect it with a Click2Call SIP trunk, which is $50 a month with 500 outbound minutes and two channels.
