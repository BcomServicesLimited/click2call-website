Australian Business Phone Statistics and Research

Original studies from real, anonymised call data across Click2Call’s Australian business customers. Every study shows its sample, method and limits, and journalists and researchers are welcome to use the figures with a link back.

Key statistics on Australian business phones

The numbers journalists and researchers ask us for most, each with its source. Our own figures come from anonymised Click2Call call data; the rest come from official Australian sources. Last updated 26 November 2026, and reviewed every quarter.

Source: Click2Call missed-call study, Mar–Nov 2026

2.81m

actively trading businesses in Australia at 30 June 2026; about 97% have fewer than 20 employees (derived from ABS counts).

How we do our research

Real data, anonymised

Studies use call records and service data from Click2Call’s own platform: times, lengths and types of calls, never recordings or transcripts. Figures are totals across many businesses. No business, caller or phone number is identified, in line with our privacy policy.

Small groups are left out

We don’t publish a figure built from fewer than ten businesses or events, so no single customer can be picked out.

Limits stated up front

Our customers are mostly Australian businesses of one to ten people. They are not a random sample of all businesses, and each study says what that means for its figures.

Method you can check

Each study states its sample size, date range and definitions. Questions about a method are welcome at support@click2call.com.au.

Using our figures

Journalists, researchers and businesses are welcome to quote, chart and republish the figures from Click2Call studies under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Please credit “Click2Call” and link to the study page. For comment or a walk-through of the data, see our press page.
