Business VoIP in Australia: What You Need to Know

What is business VoIP?

Business VoIP (voice over internet protocol) runs your business phone calls over the internet instead of a copper phone line. Your number, extensions, voicemail and call routing live in the cloud, so staff can answer on a desk phone, a computer or a mobile app anywhere in Australia, and you manage everything online.

Is Click2Call a click-to-call button for websites?

No. Click2Call is an Australian business phone provider, not a website widget. The name is a brand. If you do want a click-to-call link on your site, the Click2Call call setup API rings your phone and then connects the caller with one web request. See the call setup API guide.

How much does a business phone system cost in Australia?

With Click2Call, each Cloud PBX user is $25 a month ex GST, including a local number, 300 outbound minutes and unlimited inbound calls. Extra calls are 10c a minute to landlines and 20c to mobiles. Porting a number is $100 including GST. There are no lock-in contracts. See pricing.

Where can I find setup guides and support?

The Click2Call Help Centre has step-by-step guides for every feature, from adding a desk phone or softphone to call queues, faxmail, SMS and connecting AI voice agents. The support page has a search box and an AI support chat, and Australian staff answer email.
