Adding calling to Microsoft Teams in Australia: the short answers

Microsoft Teams can chat and hold meetings out of the box, but it cannot call ordinary phone numbers until two things are added: a Microsoft Teams Phone licence for each person, and a phone service connected to Teams. This section explains how that works in Australia, what it costs and how to do it.

What are the three ways to add calling to Teams?

  • ✓Microsoft Calling Plans: not sold in Australia. With a Calling Plan, Microsoft itself is the phone company. Microsoft’s Australia availability page lists Calling Plans as not available to purchase in Australia.
  • ✓Operator Connect: available in Australia. With Operator Connect, a phone carrier certified by Microsoft, which has already connected its network to Teams, carries the calls.
  • ✓Direct Routing: available in Australia, and how Click2Call connects. With Direct Routing, any phone provider can connect to Teams through a certified session border controller (the equipment that passes calls between a phone network and Teams).

Checked on Microsoft’s own pages on 28 November 2026: PSTN connectivity options and Availability in Australia. Microsoft also lists Teams Phone Mobile, which links a mobile SIM plan to Teams. Compare the three side by side.

Which Microsoft licence do I need?

Whichever way you connect, each person who makes and takes phone calls in Teams needs two things from Microsoft. The first is a Microsoft 365 business plan that includes Teams, such as Microsoft 365 Business Basic (AU$10.50 a user a month). The second is the Microsoft Teams Phone Standard licence (AU$15.00 a user a month), which turns on the Teams dial pad. Microsoft’s licensing page says both Direct Routing and Operator Connect require a Teams Phone licence. Both prices are Microsoft’s Australian list prices, ex GST on an annual subscription, checked on microsoft.com on 28 November 2026. These licences are bought from Microsoft; Click2Call does not sell Microsoft licences. A business that already uses Teams for chat and meetings already has the first one.
