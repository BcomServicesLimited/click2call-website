About Click2Call

Click2Call is an Australian phone company run from the Gold Coast, and part of bcom ICT, an IT services company established in 2007. We give small businesses a proper business number, from $10 a month, that rings the mobile they already have, with an AI receptionist and call summaries included. You sign up online, set it up yourself, and never sign a contract.

Last reviewed: November 2026

  • Who we are:Click2Call is a trading name of Bcom Services Pty Ltd (ABN: 92 636 893 108), an Australian-owned company based on the Gold Coast, Queensland, and part of bcom ICT, established in 2007.
  • What we sell:Australian business phone numbers (local, 04 mobile, 1300 and 1800) from $10 a month ex GST, and a full cloud phone system at $25 per user with 300 outbound minutes.
  • What is included:Call recording, AI transcripts and summaries, and an AI receptionist come with every number. Recordings and transcripts are stored in Australia.

Why We Built Click2Call

Small businesses were being offered two bad choices: an expensive phone system built for a 50-person office, or a personal mobile doing a job it was never meant for. Calls went unanswered after hours, nothing was recorded, and the business number belonged to whoever owned the handset.

Click2Call is the option in between. One business number, answered on the phones you already own, with the features a large company takes for granted: a phone menu, calls that ring the whole team, voicemail to email, and an AI receptionist for the calls you can’t get to. See how that looks in practice in our example setups.

How We Work

You set it up yourself

There is no sales call and no technician visit. Create an account, pick a number, and make a test call. If you would rather we did it, managed setup starts at $300 ex GST.

The price is the price

$10 a month for a number, or $25 a month per user with 300 minutes. Every one-off fee is on the pricing page, including the $100 to bring your existing number across.

You pay in advance, so there are no bills

Add credit, and your monthly fee and calls come out of it. Stop whenever you like.

Support from people who know the system

Written answers by email from the Australian team that runs the service, plus help guides and AI support at any hour.

Your recordings stay here

Call recordings and transcripts are stored in Australia.

Built on an Australian carrier network

The service runs on an Australian carrier network that has been carrying business calls for about ten years.
