Choosing the right plan

The Cloud PBX User ($25/month) is for anyone who needs to make and receive calls — includes a local number, 300 outbound minutes, and all Cloud PBX features.

What does it cost to migrate from an on-premise PBX to the cloud?

Usually only the porting fee. One-off costs, all optional except porting:

  • Number porting: $100 including GST per number, any type. Your number keeps working during the 5–10 business days it takes.
  • Setup: free if you do it yourself with the help guides; managed setup is $300 ex GST for up to 3 users, then $50 per extra user.
  • Handsets: optional. The softphone app is free; pre-configured Yealink desk phones are $450 and cordless $400 ex GST, posted ready to plug in.
  • Nothing else: no line rental, no PBX maintenance contract, no technician call-outs.

1300 and 1800 numbers can take up to 30 days to port. Each number you bring across then runs on its own plan ($10/month as an inbound number).

What do calls cost beyond the 300 minutes?

Calls to Australian and New Zealand numbers past the allowance are charged per minute — 10c a minute to landlines and 20c a minute to mobiles, ex GST. Calling a 1300 number is a flat 50c connection fee. If you hold a 1300 or 1800 number, inbound calls to it are 20c a minute to your account; inbound calls to local numbers are free.

Is there a cheaper VoIP-only or basic plan?

There is no stripped-down tier, because nothing is held back from the $25 user. Two things are cheaper because they do less: an Inbound Business Number at $10 a month receives calls and runs a call flow but cannot dial out, and if you already own a PBX, a SIP trunk at $50 a month supplies the lines without the phone system.
