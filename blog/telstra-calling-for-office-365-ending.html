Telstra Calling for Office 365 Ends on 30 November: What Teams Users Can Do

Telstra is withdrawing its Microsoft Teams calling service from 30 November 2026. Here is what changes, what stays the same, and how to keep making phone calls from Teams.

By Royce Clark November 25, 2026

Telstra Calling for Office 365 is the service that let Australian businesses make and take ordinary phone calls in Microsoft Teams through Telstra. Telstra stopped selling it to new customers on 1 November 2024, stopped changes to existing services on 30 August 2026, and withdraws it from 30 November 2026. In Australia it was the way Microsoft’s own Calling Plans were delivered, so businesses still using it need another way to connect Teams to the phone network.

The short version

  • 30 November 2026: Telstra Calling for Office 365 is withdrawn.
  • Teams itself doesn’t change. Chat, meetings and the Teams dial pad stay; only the company that carries your calls changes.
  • Your Teams Phone Standard licences stay. Both other options use the same Microsoft licence.
  • Two ways to keep calling: Operator Connect, through a Microsoft-certified carrier, or Direct Routing, through a provider such as Click2Call.
  • Keep your numbers by porting them. A port usually takes 5 to 10 business days, so start now and ask Telstra what happens to your numbers in the meantime.

What is Telstra Calling for Office 365?

Microsoft Teams can chat, meet and video call out of the box, but it can’t ring an ordinary landline or mobile on its own. For that, Teams needs a Teams Phone Standard licence and a carrier to connect it to the phone network. Microsoft offers its own Calling Plans only in some markets. In Australia, they were delivered through Telstra, as Telstra Calling for Office 365.

When does Telstra Calling for Office 365 end?

According to Telstra’s critical information summary for the service:

  • 1 November 2024: closed to new customers.
  • 30 August 2026: existing services can no longer be added to, changed or recontracted.
  • 30 November 2026: the service is withdrawn.

If your business still uses it, ask Telstra directly what happens to your calls and numbers on and after 30 November, and confirm the dates for your account.

What are the options for calling from Microsoft Teams in Australia?

There are two ways to put real phone calls into Teams for an Australian business today. Both keep the same Teams dial pad and the same Teams Phone Standard licence.

  • Operator Connect. A Microsoft-certified carrier connects to your Teams tenant, and you manage numbers in the Teams admin centre. In Australia the certified carriers include Telstra, Optus, Symbio and Vocus. It usually provides calls only, with pricing set by the carrier.
  • Direct Routing. A provider connects its own phone network to Teams. With Click2Call, a full business phone system sits behind Teams: call recording, AI transcription and summaries, call queues, an auto-attendant and voicemail to email, with recordings stored in Australia.

Can I keep my phone numbers?

Yes, by porting them to the new provider. Australian local, 1300 and 1800 numbers can all be ported. With Click2Call a port costs a flat $100 including GST per number and usually takes 5 to 10 business days. Because of that lead time, start the port as early as you can, and check with Telstra how your numbers are handled until the port completes.
