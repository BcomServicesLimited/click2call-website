How Much Does a Business Phone System Cost in Australia?

What a business phone system costs in Australia: $10 a month for a number, $25 a month per person on a cloud system, one-off costs, team examples and fees to check in any quote.

Reviewed by Royce Clark, Director · Last checked 27 November 2026

By Royce Clark • Updated 27 Nov 2026

A cloud business phone system in Australia costs from about $10 a month for a number that receives calls, or about $25 a month for each person who makes and receives calls. That is before GST, with no hardware to buy. A team of five on Click2Call is $135 a month ex GST, including a main number and 300 outgoing minutes for each person. The main other costs are one-off: moving existing numbers across, desk phones if you want them, and set-up help if you don’t do it yourself.

What one-off costs should I budget for?

  • Moving your numbers. Porting is $100 inc GST per number, charged once the port succeeds. A new local number is free; a new 04 mobile number is $50 inc GST, and a new 1300 or 1800 number is $100 inc GST.
  • Desk phones, if you want them. They are optional: the iPhone and Android apps and the Web Phone in a computer’s browser are free. Pre-configured Yealink desk phones are $450 ex GST each, or you can use compatible phones you already own.
  • Set-up. Setting up yourself is free. Done-for-you set-up is $300 ex GST for 1 to 3 lines, and free on approval for 4 or more Business Lines or Teams Lines once the first month is paid.

There are no connection fees, no hardware leases and no technician visits.

What does a traditional on-premise phone system cost?

An on-premise PBX is a phone system in a box in your office, connected to phone lines. Its costs come in five parts, and the hardware price alone understates the total:

  1. The PBX unit, bought outright and sized to the number of lines and extensions.
  2. Handsets for every person, compatible with that PBX.
  3. Installation and programming by a technician, who comes back for every change.
  4. Phone lines, rented monthly whether you use them or not. These are now a SIP trunk over the internet, because ISDN and copper lines have closed with the NBN.
  5. A maintenance contract, usually yearly, plus replacement when the hardware reaches end of life.

If you already own a PBX that works, a SIP trunk keeps it running: Click2Call’s is $50 a month ex GST with 500 minutes and 2 channels, and extra channels are $3 a month each. If the PBX is due for replacement, a cloud system usually costs less than new hardware plus a maintenance contract. See cloud PBX vs a traditional phone system.

What extra costs should I check for in any quote?

Business phone pricing often looks cheaper per line than it is. Before comparing providers, check each quote for:

  • Contract length and what it costs to leave early.
  • Minimum users, such as a two-user minimum on a per-user plan.
  • Charges for incoming calls on local numbers, or minute packs that count incoming calls.
  • Number fees: allocation fees for new numbers, and fees to port numbers in or out.
  • Add-ons sold separately, such as call recording, transcripts, queues or AI answering.
  • Hardware leases that run longer than the phone contract.
  • Calls to 13 and 1300 numbers and international calls. At Click2Call, calling a 1300 number is a flat 50c a call.
  • Incoming call charges on 1300 and 1800 numbers. These are 20c a minute at Click2Call, because the business pays part or all of the call.
  • Microsoft licences, if calls run in Teams.

How does billing work?

Click2Call is prepaid. You add credit by card, from $10, and monthly fees and calls come out of it. There are no bills, no contract and no annual plan, so there’s no annual discount either. If credit runs out, incoming calls keep working and calls out pause until you top up. Turning on auto top-up stops that happening. GST of 10% applies to all charges, and tax invoices show it separately.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The 7-day free trial includes $11 of credit, enough to set up a local number and make test calls, and no card is needed to sign up. Every new account is checked by a person before activation, usually within the hour during business hours.

Key terms

Cloud PBX (hosted phone system)
A business phone system that runs in the provider’s data centre and is managed in a web portal.
On-premise PBX
A phone system installed in your office and connected to phone lines.
Line
A plan for one person to make and receive calls on their own number.
Extension
An internal destination, such as a person’s app or desk phone, with no public number of its own.
Channel
One call in progress. Click2Call accounts start with 2, with one more for each Business Line or Teams Line, and extra channels are $3 a month.
Porting
Moving an existing number to a new provider without changing it.
Ex GST / inc GST
Price before or after the 10% goods and services tax.
